Homebase: stay close,
stay yourself.

A family app that shows where everyone is, lets you message, and makes calls that really ring. Nobody should feel watched: the person being located always sees exactly what is shared, can pause it, and chooses what parents can do.

What the family seesWhere you are while sharing is on, your battery level, and the messages you send.
What it never doesHide itself, read your other apps or messages, turn on your mic or camera by itself, or lock your device.
You are in controlPause any time, set quiet hours, say "not now" to a location request, and switch off any parent permission from your own panel.

Setup: download the app, allow installs once, a parent creates the family, then everyone else joins with an 8-character code.
