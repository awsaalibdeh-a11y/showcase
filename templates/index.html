Featured · Android, Windows and web

Homebase: stay close,
stay yourself.

A family app that shows where everyone is, lets you message, and makes calls that ring in their own window. Parents see each child's screen time today and can help with limits. Nobody should feel watched: the person being located always sees exactly what is shared, can pause it, and chooses what parents can do.

What the family seesWhere you are while sharing is on, your battery, your screen time today (minutes playing or watching, never homework), and the messages you send.
What parents can doLock a device (never more than 4 hours a day) or ground certain apps with a minute's warning. Every lock and grounding is announced in the family chat.
What it never doesHide itself, read your other apps or messages, turn on your mic or camera by itself, or secretly change your screen time.
You are in controlPause any time, set quiet hours, say "not now" to a location request, and switch off any parent permission from your own panel.
